Source: MCC NEET-PG 2026 provisional allotment results for Round 1, Round 2, Round 3 and the Stray Vacancy round (labelled Round 4 here). Each round's column shows closing rank (bold, the highest/worst allotted rank) with opening rank and seats allotted underneath, for that exact college + branch + category + quota combination; a round with no matching allotment shows —. Round 1 and Round 4 are each that round's own full allotment list. Round 2 and Round 3 are the effective seat held after that round: a candidate's own fresh allotment for that round where one exists, otherwise their earlier-round seat carried forward if the round file's status marked it "Reported" — a seat marked "Not Reported", "Seat Surrendered" or "Seat Cancelled" is dropped rather than carried forward. College names are re-cased from the source files' mixed/all-caps text into standard title case. City is parsed from the institute's address and general knowledge of the institution; a city with a dashed underline and ~ is a best-effort guess from messy address text, and a small remainder still shows — where none could be inferred.
