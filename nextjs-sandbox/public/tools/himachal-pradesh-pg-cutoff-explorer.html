About this data. Source: Atal Medical & Research University (AMRU), Himachal Pradesh, final seat allocation lists for MD/MS/DNB 2025-26: Round 1 (24 Nov 2025), Round 2 (20 Dec 2025), Round 3 (16 Feb 2026), stray round (24 Feb 2026) and 2nd stray round (27 Feb 2026). AIR (All India Rank) is taken from the AMRU merit list of each round, matched by application number. SML is the Merit No. printed in that round’s allotment list; AMRU revises the merit list every round (candidates are added or removed), so the same candidate can carry a different number in different rounds. Each list shows only candidates newly allotted or upgraded in that round. New allotment counts those candidates; All-round counts everyone holding a seat in that group after the round (a candidate who is upgraded leaves the earlier seat). Closing is the last-ranked candidate in the group. Each row is one college, branch, quota and category: State Quota (HP Quota - Direct) and In Service (HP Quota In-service) are the state quotas, All India Quota (Management Quota) and NRI are the private-college quotas; NRI seats show no category. Single Girl Child and PwD are shown as special reservations. No seat matrix was available, so seat counts are not shown. Verify with the current AMRU notice.
