About this tab. These are the seats that do not require a domicile of the state, so candidates from any state can apply: Andhra Pradesh Other (S1A) and NRI (S2); Bihar and Uttar Pradesh private-college seats, all categories; Karnataka Open (OPN), Management (MNG) and NRI; Telangana Management (MQ1) and NRI (MQ2); West Bengal Private management and NRI. Uttar Pradesh in-service seats are left out. Quotas are grouped into four: NRI (all NRI quotas of every state together), Muslim minority and Muslim minority NRI (kept separate), and Management, which combines all the remaining seats: Andhra Pradesh Other (S1A), Bihar and Uttar Pradesh private-college seats, Karnataka Open and Management, Telangana MQ1 and West Bengal Private management; where a college and branch has more than one of them, the figures are combined (allotted counts added, closing AIR is the highest). Each row is one college, branch, quota and category from that state’s own allotment lists, so the State column tells you which counselling it belongs to. Closing AIR is the last-ranked candidate in the group (NEET-PG All India Rank). New allotment counts only candidates newly allotted in that round (including upgrades); All-round counts everyone on that round’s list; Karnataka does not publish list counts, so Allotted shows a dash in All-round view. Round numbers follow each state’s own lists (hover a cell to see the round name): Andhra Pradesh Phase 1 to 3 and stray; Bihar, Uttar Pradesh and Karnataka Rounds 1 to 3, stray (Karnataka also stray extended); Telangana Rounds 1 to 6; West Bengal Rounds 1 to 3, stray vacancy and revised stray. Seat counts and state merit numbers are not shown on this tab. Classification of college type and city for West Bengal is by ASMI. Verify with each state’s official notice before relying on it.
