Source. MCC final seat matrices for PG Counselling 2026 (MD/MS): All India Quota, Deemed Universities and Central Internal Quota, loaded for Round 1; 31,708 seats in total. Seat groups: AIQ = All India Quota seats in all institutes (including central university institutes and DNB-type hospitals); Deemed University = paid, NRI and minority seats in deemed universities; Central University = central internal quota seats (Delhi University, IP University, BHU and AMU quotas); DNB = DNB Quota seats (NBEMS) in government and private hospitals. Category columns count seats reserved for that category (Open, OBC, EWS, SC, ST); the PwD column shows the seats among them reserved for candidates with benchmark disability, which are already included in each category column. Seat numbers change between rounds, so confirm against the official MCC notice before deciding.
