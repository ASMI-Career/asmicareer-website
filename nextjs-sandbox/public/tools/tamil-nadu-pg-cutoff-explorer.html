About this data. Source: Tamil Nadu NEET PG 2025 Management Quota provisional allotment lists, Round 1 (26 Nov 2025), Round 2 (23 Dec 2025), Round 3 (16 Feb 2026) and Round 4 (24 Feb 2026). SML is the G Rank printed in the list and AIR is the matching NEET PG All India Rank from the ASMI TN SML-AIR sheet (for seven closing ranks the sheet has no AIR, so AIR shows a dash); where two candidates share a rank the letter suffix (A, B, ...) is ignored. Quota and category follow the Management Quota categories (TN Management Quota: GEN, NRI, Telugu, Malayalam and Christian Minority; CMC Vellore: General Merit, Institutional Preference, Minority Network, Service); hover a category to see who is eligible. New allotment counts candidates newly allotted in that round (fresh or upgraded); All-round also counts candidates who kept their seat (“Retained”). Seat counts are not published in these lists, so Seats is the number of candidates allotted.
