About this data. Source: Dr. N.T.R. University of Health Sciences, Andhra Pradesh, college-wise final allotment lists for PG (Medical) Management Quota 2025-26: Phase I (10 Dec 2025), Phase II (28 Dec 2025), Phase III mop-up (16 Feb 2026) and the stray round. The allotment lists show NEET-PG rank (All India Rank, AIR). The State Merit List number (SML) comes from the university’s consolidated final merit list (3,139 candidates), matched to each allotted candidate by NEET roll number; every Round 1 to 3 candidate was found, and 26 stray-round candidates are not on that list, so their SML is blank. SML is shown under the closing AIR as the number of the last-ranked candidate. Each list includes candidates who kept an earlier seat. New allotment counts only candidates newly allotted to that seat in that round (including upgrades); All-round counts everyone on that round’s list. Closing AIR is the last-ranked candidate in the group. Allotted is the number of candidates. Seats come from the seat matrix published for each round (Round 2 includes enhanced seats). The seat matrix has no category split, and neither does this table: each row is one college, branch and quota, and Closing AIR is the last-ranked candidate in it across all categories. In Round 4 (stray) Seats is the list of vacant seats announced; not-reported seats were also filled, so allotments can exceed it. In the stray round the PDF gives one combined Local + Other (S1) list; those allotments are counted under Other (S1A). FIMS and NIMRA print one combined Other + Local figure, shown on both. Verify with the current Dr. N.T.R. UHS notice.
