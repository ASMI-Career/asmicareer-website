About this data. Source: Dr. N.T.R. University of Health Sciences, Andhra Pradesh, college-wise final allotment lists for PG (Medical) Management Quota 2025-26: Phase I (10 Dec 2025), Phase II (28 Dec 2025), Phase III mop-up (16 Feb 2026) and the stray round. The lists show NEET-PG rank only, which is the All India Rank (AIR); there is no separate state merit number. Each list includes candidates who kept an earlier seat. New allotment counts only candidates newly allotted to that seat in that round (including upgrades); All-round counts everyone on that round’s list. Closing AIR is the last-ranked candidate in the group. Allotted is the number of candidates. Seats come from the seat matrix published for each round (Round 2 includes enhanced seats). A seat matrix has no category split, so Seats is the number of seats in that quota for all categories together, and the same figure appears on every category row of that quota. In Round 4 (stray) Seats is the list of vacant seats announced; not-reported seats were also filled, so allotments can exceed it. In the stray round Local (S1B) seats are treated as Other (S1A), so they appear as S1. FIMS and NIMRA print one combined Other + Local figure, shown on both. Verify with the current Dr. N.T.R. UHS notice.
