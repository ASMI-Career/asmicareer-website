About this data. Source: Chhattisgarh NEET PG (MD/MS) 2025 counselling by the Directorate of Medical Education, Chhattisgarh: provisional allotment lists for Round 1, Round 2, Round 3 (mop-up), Round 4 (stray) and Round 5 (extended stray), the merit lists for each round and the clear-vacancy seat matrices. The allotment lists show NEET-PG rank (All India Rank, AIR) and the Chhattisgarh State Rank (SML); both were matched against each round’s merit list (every allotted candidate was found, with the same AIR and state rank). Each list also shows candidates who kept their seat (marked “Not Upgraded”). New allotment counts only candidates newly allotted in that round (fresh allotments and upgrades); All-round counts everyone on that round’s list. Closing AIR is the last-ranked candidate in the group and SML is that candidate’s state rank in that round. Allotted is the number of candidates. Seats are the clear vacancies announced for each round (Round 2 includes the one extra MD Paediatrics State seat added at Raigarh on 31 Dec 2025); no seat matrix was available for Round 1, so Round 1 Seats is the number of seats allotted in Round 1. Allotments can exceed the announced vacancy because seats vacated by upgrades are re-allotted in the same round. 14 candidates in Round 3 are listed only as retaining their Round 2 admission with no earlier seat on record, so they are not counted in any seat group. Other State is a separate quota made of candidates marked “NON CG Domicile and MBBS Pass out other than NMC-recognised Medical College of Chhattisgarh” who were allotted State Open seats, so their cutoff is not mixed with Chhattisgarh candidates; the seat matrix does not split seats by candidate type, so Other State shows Seats only for Round 1 (seats allotted) and the State Open rows still carry the full announced vacancy. Non-Chhattisgarh candidates allotted NRI seats stay in the NRI quotas. Each row is one college, branch, quota and category (UR includes UR-Female). College type (government or private) is ASMI’s classification. Verify with the official notice before relying on it.
