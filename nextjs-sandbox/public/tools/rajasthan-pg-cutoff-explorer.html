About this data. Source: Rajasthan State Medical PG counselling (NEET PG Admission/Counselling Board 2025, SMS Medical College, Jaipur) provisional allotment lists: Round 1 (revised, 7 Dec 2025), Round 2 (26 Dec 2025), Round 3 (revised, 16 Feb 2026) and the stray vacancy round (25 Feb 2026). AIR (NEET-PG All India Rank) is taken from the provisional merit lists, matched by NEET roll number. SML is the Combined State Merit No. printed in that round’s allotment list; the list is revised every round, so the same candidate can carry a different number in different rounds. New allotment counts candidates newly allotted or upgraded in that round (Round 3 uses the Fresh and Upgrade status in the list; in Round 2 a candidate counts as new when the seat differs from the one held before); All-round counts everyone holding a seat in that group after the round (a candidate who is upgraded leaves the earlier seat). Closing AIR is the last-ranked candidate in the group. Each row is one college, branch, quota and category. Quota is the seat type printed in the list: Government is shown as State Quota, AI as All India Quota and Management as Management Quota. Category is the category the seat was allotted under in Rounds 1, 2 and the stray round. The Round 3 list prints only the candidate’s provisional category, so for a seat a candidate already held the earlier allotted category is carried forward, and for Round 3 fresh and upgraded allotments the provisional category is used. Degree is MD/MS, DNB or Diploma as printed. College type is classified by ASMI. No seat matrix was available, so seat counts are not shown. Verify with the current Rajasthan counselling notice.
