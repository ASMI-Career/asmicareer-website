About this data. Source: Haryana PG (Medical) counselling allotment lists 2025-26 for Round 1, Round 2, Round 3 and the stray round (Round 4). The lists show the NEET-PG rank (All India Rank, AIR). SML (State Merit List number) is the candidate’s position by NEET rank among all candidates shown as eligible in the four Haryana merit lists (Rounds 1 to 4, 2,616 candidates), so it is the same in every round; the S.No printed in each round’s list restarts every round and is not used. No seat matrix was published, so seat counts are not shown. Rounds 2 to 4 list only candidates newly allotted or upgraded in that round. New allotment counts those candidates; All-round counts everyone holding a seat in that group after the round (a candidate who is upgraded leaves the earlier seat). Closing AIR is the last-ranked candidate in the group. Each row is one college, branch, quota, category and special-reservation combination; Quota and Category are kept separate, as in Uttar Pradesh. Quota is taken from the category printed in the list: Mgmt is All India Quota, NRI is NRI, Minority is Muslim Minority Quota, and every other category is State Quota. IP, IS, PwD and ESM FF are shown exactly as printed; the lists do not spell out IP and IS. Candidates whose seat category was converted in Round 3 or the stray round are shown under the category they were allotted. Verify with the current Haryana counselling notice.
