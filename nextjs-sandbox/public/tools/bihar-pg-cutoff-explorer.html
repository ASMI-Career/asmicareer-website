About this data. Source: Bihar PG Medical Admission Counselling (PGMAC) 2025 allotment results: Round 1 (revised, 28 Nov 2025), Round 2 (revised, 26 Dec 2025), Round 3 (re-revised, 21 Feb 2026) and the stray vacancy round (26 Feb 2026). The lists show NEET-PG rank (All India Rank, AIR). Each list includes candidates who kept an earlier seat. New allotment counts only candidates newly allotted to that seat in that round (fresh allotments and upgrades); All-round counts everyone on that round’s list, including those who retained a seat. Closing AIR is the last-ranked candidate in the group. Allotted is the number of candidates. Each row is one college, branch and seat category (UR, EBC, BC, SC, ST, EWS, RCG, DQ, NRI, MM, SM, shown as printed in the lists; the lists do not explain the NRI, MM, SM, RCG and DQ codes). Seat counts are not shown because no seat matrix was available. Government or private is ASMI’s classification: colleges that allot NRI, MM or SM seats are shown as private, except Madhubani Medical College, which is government. Verify with the official notice before relying on it. Government-college seats reserved for women (marked Female in the allotment lists, 115 seat groups) are shown separately from the general seats, so their cutoffs are not mixed; use the Gender filter to see only Female seats or only the general seats. Seats come from the Bihar seat matrix (Round 1) and the vacancy lists published before Rounds 2, 3 and the stray round, by college, branch, category and open or female seat. Allotments can exceed the seat figure because of compensation, jump-over and converted seats and seats vacated by upgrades during a round; a dash means the college and branch are not in that round's list.
